Delete your Axiscript account

Last updated November 25, 2026

You can delete your Axiscript account yourself, or ask us to do it.

In the app

  1. Open Axiscript and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. If you want a copy of your work first, tap Export my data. You get one zip file with your reports, projects, mileage and photos.
  4. Tap Delete account, then Yes, delete my account.

If you can't open the app

Email support@axiscript.com from the email address on your account and say you want your account deleted. We may reply to confirm it is you. We finish the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Your backed-up reports, projects, mileage records, settings, saved Ask Chief conversations, and feedback records.
  • Your uploaded files and photos.

Deleting in the app takes effect right away and can't be undone.

What we keep

  • Backup copies of our database, which are removed on their normal cycle.
  • Limited records the law requires, such as billing records.
  • Emails you sent to support@axiscript.com, unless you ask us to remove them.
  • Anything already on your own device. Remove the app, or clear its storage, to remove it.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or Apple. Cancel it in the store first so you aren't charged again.
