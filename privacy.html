Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains what Axiscript LLC ("Axiscript," "we," "us") collects through the Axiscript app, the web app at app.axiscript.com, any other phone, tablet, or laptop version, and axiscript.com (the "Service"), how we use it, and your choices.

The short version

  • Your reports belong to you. We never sell your data, and it isn't used to train AI.
  • Accounts are open to testers in the United States.
  • We collect what we need to run the Service: your account, the reports, service stops, photos, and mileage you create, Command Center project information if you use it, and location when you allow it.
  • We don't sell your information and don't show ads.
  • To write reports and to run other AI features, our servers send the relevant information to our AI provider, Anthropic. Anthropic doesn't train its models on the data we send it.
  • Each account can reach only its own records. If you are invited to test Command Center, that project data is visible only to your account and is never used in marketing.
  • Your data is stored in the United States.
  • You can export your data or delete your account anytime in the app (Account screen), on the Delete your account page, or at support@axiscript.com, even if your membership is inactive.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and the app has no advertising ID.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never in plain text), and profile details you add, such as company name or logo. You can create an account to test the Service. It is available in the United States only for now.
  • Your Content: reports, notes, photos and photo notes, specs and forms you upload, project, client, and contractor details, mileage records, Distribution service stops, and Command Center project information. A service stop can include the stop details, your signature, the arrival position and work spot, and stop photos. Stops and stop photos sync to our servers and show on your other devices. Your reports and stops may name other people and companies, such as clients, contractors, and crew.
  • Location, only with your device's permission:
    • Photo stamps: the GPS location and time saved with each photo, used to stamp it.
    • Service stops: when you use a Distribution service stop, the app can save your arrival position and the work spot on that stop. These are backed up to your account and show on your other devices.
    • Mileage tracker: your location while a trip is running, used to calculate the miles. The app shows a notification while a trip is running, and tracking stops when you tap Stop. The coordinates stay on your device. What is backed up is the trip's date, start and end time, miles, and any project tag or note you add.
    • Job address and weather: when you ask for them, your device's coordinates are turned into a street address by your phone's own location service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) and sent to our weather provider, Open-Meteo, to fill in the weather. We don't send your name or email with that request. A service stop also stores the job-site street address in your account.
    • You can turn location off anytime in your device settings. Features that need it won't work fully without it.
  • Voice-to-text: uses your device's built-in speech recognition and is set to run on the device. We receive the text, not your voice recording.
  • Ask Chief conversations: your questions and Chief's answers are saved in your account so you can come back to them. You can delete them in the app.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the app version, your device model, the screen you were on, your account email, and a picture if you attach one. It is sent to support@axiscript.com.
  • Membership details: your plan and renewal status from Apple, Google, or our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Waitlist: if you join our waitlist on axiscript.com, your email address and, if you add them, your name, type of work, and how you send your daily reports.
  • Technical information needed to run and secure the Service, such as app version, device type, and server logs (which may include IP addresses). We don't use advertising or analytics trackers. The app does not currently send crash reports. If we add crash reporting, we will update this policy first, and it will not include your email or the text of your reports.

2. How we use it

To run the Service (save, back up, and sync your reports, service stops, and photos, stamp photos, track mileage, fill in weather, create PDFs, and operate Command Center), to have Chief draft your reports and read ticket and schedule photos, to manage your account and membership, to answer support requests and send service messages (such as billing, renewal reminders, and policy changes), to keep the Service secure, to fix and improve it, and to follow the law.

We may use the edits you make to Chief's drafts to improve Chief's results for you, within your account. We don't use your content to improve results for other users or to train anyone's AI models.

We may publish totals that don't identify anyone, such as total reports written across all users. We do not use tester or Command Center project data in marketing.

3. AI processing

When Chief writes or rewrites a report, or when another AI feature (including Command Center features that use AI) needs a draft or a reading, our servers send the relevant notes, report text, specs, project information, and any photos or scanned pages you ask Chief to read (such as handwritten notes, field logs, tally sheets, and the markout ticket and schedule photos you photograph for a Distribution service stop) to Anthropic to generate the result. When web access is on for Ask Chief, Anthropic's web search and fetch tools may look up public sources, such as OSHA or PHMSA pages, to answer your question. Anthropic processes this data as our service provider under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on that data. Don't put information into Axiscript that your client or employer doesn't allow to be processed by AI or cloud services.

We also share information when you choose to, such as when you send or share a PDF, and when the law requires it or to protect people's safety or rights. If Axiscript is sold or merged, information may move to the new owner under this policy. Companies that give their people a referral discount get no access to anyone's account or reports.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored and processed in the United States. Data moving between the app or web app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Each account can reach only its own records; we enforce that with access rules in the database. Notes saved offline stay on your device until they sync. Reports, service stops, and photos you choose to back up are stored in your account on our database and file storage. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content while your account exists, including while your membership is inactive, so your reports are there when you come back. If you delete your account in the app, your account, your backed-up reports, service stops and stop photos, projects, mileage and settings, and your uploaded files and photos are removed right away from our active systems. If you ask us by email, we do it within 30 days. Backup copies are removed on their normal cycle. Copies already on your own device stay there until you remove the app, and emails you sent to support stay in our support mailbox unless you ask us to remove them. We may keep limited records the law requires, such as billing records.

7. Your choices and rights

Everyone, wherever they live, can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete their information. You can export your data or delete your account in the app (Account screen), or email support@axiscript.com from your account email. If you can't open the app, the steps are also on the Delete your account page. You can also export reports as PDFs in the app, even while your membership is inactive. We may need to verify your identity, and we won't treat you differently for making a request. You can turn off location or the mileage tracker anytime, and unsubscribe from non-essential emails.

8. Command Center testers

If you are invited to test Command Center, your Command Center project data is visible only to your account. It is not shared with other users, and it is never used in marketing.

9. Children

The Service is for working professionals 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Website and web app

axiscript.com and app.axiscript.com don't use advertising or tracking cookies or analytics. Our website host, GitHub Pages, and our web-app host, Cloudflare Pages, may log visitor IP addresses for security.

11. Changes

If we make material changes, we'll tell you by email or in the app at least 30 days before they take effect, unless the law requires a change sooner.
