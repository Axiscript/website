FAQ

Common questions.

Axiscript is an AI-powered field operations, documentation, and reporting platform. Chief is the integrated assistant for documentation, reporting, and technical questions.

What is Axiscript?

Axiscript brings field documentation, daily reporting, progress tracking, job records, and AI-powered assistance together in one platform. It helps you organize work, cut repetitive paperwork, and keep project information accessible, on the job or back at the desk. Works on your phone, tablet, and laptop.

What is Chief?

Chief is Axiscript’s own field assistant, built for inspection work. Type or talk your notes, and he drafts the daily in an inspector’s voice for you to review. He can also fill your client’s daily form and answer questions about procedures, specifications, code, or safety. He does not replace the project specifications, your judgment, or the qualified person on site.

Is Chief just ChatGPT?

No. Chief is Axiscript’s own field assistant, built for inspection work. Chief uses AI, but answers are grounded in Axiscript’s field library and your project documents. He follows a source order, can cite what he used, and is not a general chatbot.

What is in Chief’s knowledge library?

The library holds public federal regulations from 11 sources, including OSHA and PHMSA, plus the project documents you upload. Licensed standards, such as API or ASME, are not included. Manufacturer documents are not in the library yet.

Does Ask Chief use my specifications?

If you have uploaded client specifications, it uses them, says so, and can cite them. If not, it gives general guidance and tells you that.

Does Chief replace my judgment or the project specifications?

No. Chief is an assistant. He does not replace the project specifications, your professional judgment, or the qualified person on site.

Does Chief search the web?

Only when web access is on. Chief always starts with your project documents and Axiscript’s field library. When web access is on, he can then check official sources such as OSHA, PHMSA, eCFR, and approved manufacturer pages, and after that the general web, which he labels as supplemental. Forums are blocked. Each point is labeled a requirement, a manufacturer recommendation, or general guidance. He flags conflicts, lists sources with dates, and says when he cannot verify. When web access is off, he stays with your documents and the library.

Are my Chief questions used to train AI models?

No. We do not use your questions or other content to train anyone’s AI models, or to improve results for other users. We may use the edits you make to Chief’s drafts to improve Chief’s results for you, inside your account only.

What devices can I use?

A phone (iPhone or Android), a tablet, or a laptop browser at app.axiscript.com. On the laptop, the camera and mic stay off. Upload photos from this device, and type your notes.
