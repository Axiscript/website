FAQ

Common questions.

Axiscript is an AI-powered field operations, documentation, and reporting platform. Chief is the integrated assistant for documentation, reporting, and technical questions.

How do I get Axiscript?

Axiscript is in open testing on Google Play in the United States. Install it from the Google Play listing, or sign in at app.axiscript.com. iPhone and iPad are coming soon.

What does it cost?

Testing is free. Paid plans start at launch: $19.99 a month or $199 a year, each with a 14-day free trial.

How does a Distribution service stop work?

Photograph the daily schedule and Chief pulls your rows into today’s route. At each stop you Arrive, confirm the markout ticket, Inspect the work, and Close Out. Submit and you get a service record PDF.

Do my stops and photos sync?

Yes. Stops and photos save on the device first, then sync across your phone, tablet, and the web app when you have signal. You need to be signed in.

What is Axiscript?

Axiscript brings field documentation, daily reporting, job records, and AI-powered assistance together in one platform. Today that means Distribution service stops, Pipeline dailies, Ask Chief, and mileage. It works on your phone, tablet, and the web app.

What is Chief?

Chief is Axiscript’s own field assistant, built for inspection work. He writes the Pipeline daily from your notes in an inspector’s voice, reads schedule photos and markout tickets, writes the narrative on a service stop, and answers questions about procedures, specifications, code, or safety. You review everything before it goes out.

Is Chief just ChatGPT?

No. Chief is Axiscript’s own field assistant, built for inspection work. Chief uses AI, but answers are grounded in Axiscript’s field library and your project documents. He follows a source order, can cite what he used, and is not a general chatbot.

What is in Chief’s knowledge library?

The library holds public federal regulations from 11 sources, including OSHA and PHMSA, plus the project documents you upload. Licensed standards, such as API or ASME, are not included. Manufacturer documents are not in the library yet.

Does Ask Chief use my specifications?

If you have uploaded client specifications, it uses them, says so, and can cite them. If not, it gives general guidance and tells you that.

Does Chief replace my judgment or the project specifications?

No. Chief is an assistant. He does not replace the project specifications, your professional judgment, or the qualified person on site.

Does Chief search the web?

Yes, when a question needs it. Chief starts with your project documents and Axiscript’s field library. If that is not enough, he checks official sources such as OSHA, PHMSA, and eCFR, then the wider web, which he labels as supplemental. While he works you see each step, such as Checking the library or Searching the web. He lists his sources and says when he cannot verify something.

Are my Chief questions used to train AI models?

No. We do not use your questions or other content to train anyone’s AI models, or to improve results for other users. We may use the edits you make to Chief’s drafts to improve Chief’s results for you, inside your account only.

What devices can I use?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play, and any laptop or tablet browser at app.axiscript.com. iPhone and iPad are coming soon. On the laptop the camera and mic stay off, so upload photos and type your notes.
