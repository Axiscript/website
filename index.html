Your notes
Onsite 0630. Day 1 Blackridge Phase 1. Met with super Mercer and GF Holt at staging yard. JSA covered unloading equipment, clearing, overhead hazards and heavy equipment. One calls and ROW limits reviewed.

Contractor started moving equipment in around 0700. 2 D6s, 2 CAT 336s, CAT 320, 850L dozer, 299 skid steer and forestry mulcher. All unloaded at staging area near sta 0+00. Equipment and staging area looked fine.

Survey crew staking centerline and ROW limits sta 0+00 to 35+00. Existing utilities marked near access road sta 4+80. Survey still working ahead.

Clearing started around 0830 at sta 0+00. Got about 1250' cleared today, mostly brush and small timber. Bigger trees around sta 11+00 left for later. Grubbed first 600'. Dozers rough graded sta 0+00 to 8+00. Wet ground around sta 6+50, crew put timber mats down for access. No major rock so far.

Environmental crew installed 900' silt fence along downslope side sta 2+00 to 11+00. Straw wattles placed near drainage at sta 9+75. Environmental coordinator checked work, no issues noted.

First pipe delivery around 1030. 2 trucks, 40 joints 12" X52 steel, .375 wall, avg 40' each. 1600' total delivered to laydown yard. Unloaded with excavator and lifting attachments. Pipe set on timber skids and chocked. Checked several joints, coating looked good, no visible damage. Heat numbers and markings on pipe.

Clearing and grading continued through afternoon. Clearing ended sta 12+50, grading reached 8+00. No stringing, ditching, welding or pipe installed today. Equipment and material yard secured. Offsite 1730.

Safety:
The inspector arrived on site at 6:30 a.m. and met with Supervisor Mercer and General Foreman Holt at the staging yard. The JSA covered unloading equipment, clearing, overhead hazards, and heavy equipment. One-calls and ROW limits were reviewed.

Clearing/Grading:
The contractor began moving equipment in at approximately 7:00 a.m. and unloaded it at the staging area near Sta. 0+00. No visible concerns were noted at the equipment or staging area.
• Two (2) D6s
• Two (2) CAT 336s
• CAT 320
• 850L dozer
• 299 skid steer
• Forestry mulcher

The survey crew staked the centerline and ROW limits from Sta. 0+00 to Sta. 35+00. Existing utilities were marked near the access road at Sta. 4+80. The survey crew continued working ahead.

Clearing began at approximately 8:30 a.m. at Sta. 0+00 and consisted mostly of brush and small timber. Larger trees around Sta. 11+00 were left for later. Clearing and grading continued through the afternoon, and dozers rough graded the cleared ROW.
• Approximately 1,250' cleared, ending at Sta. 12+50
• First 600' grubbed
• Rough grading from Sta. 0+00 to Sta. 8+00

The environmental crew installed silt fence along the downslope side and placed straw wattles near the drainage. The environmental coordinator checked the work, and no visible issues were noted.
• Silt fence: 900' from Sta. 2+00 to Sta. 11+00
• Straw wattles: near drainage at Sta. 9+75

No stringing, ditching, welding, or pipe installation was performed today.

Site Conditions:
Ground was wet near Sta. 6+50, and the crew placed timber mats for access. No major rock was encountered.

Material:
The first pipe delivery arrived at approximately 10:30 a.m. The pipe was unloaded to the laydown yard with an excavator and lifting attachments, then set on timber skids and chocked. Several joints were inspected, and no visible damage to the coating was observed. Heat numbers and markings were present on the pipe.
• Two (2) trucks
• Forty (40) joints of 12" X52 steel pipe, .375 wall, averaging 40' each
• 1,600' total delivered

The equipment and material yard was secured. The inspector departed the site at 5:30 p.m.
