Meet Chief

Notes in. Report out.

Meet Chief. He writes your reports. Type it the way you’d say it in the truck. Chief turns those notes into a client-ready daily report, flags what doesn’t add up or what’s missing, and updates progress against the job. You review, edit, and approve every word. End the night with the report done — minutes, not an hour.

Midstream · 12" gathering line

Notes in

onsite 0600 jsa w/ tie-in crew + 2 weld rigs. 12in gathering line sta 142+50 to 151+10. 9 mainline welds today, welders 07 and 12. W-118 W-119 W-120 W-121 W-122 W-123 W-124 W-125. ut shot 6, 2 cut out for porosity, rewelded. holiday test 900 ft, 3 holidays repaired, coating batch D-4418. lowered in 600 ft, padding sand 6in. ditch 5ft avg, crossed foreign 8in at 147+30, 24in sep. rained 1300-1400 shut down. off 1730

Report out
What Chief caught

Your notes say 9 mainline welds today, but 8 weld numbers are listed (W-118 through W-125). Which is right?

9 mainline welds 8 welds (W-118–W-125)
Integrity Check

The two rewelds don’t have NDE results yet. Add them, or note they’re pending.

Progress updated
Today · 600 ft 3,420 / 9,200 ft · 37%

Notes in

onsite 0700. tailgate w/ mike, 4 man crew. 1" pe service to 412 elm st off the 4" pe main. saddle-fused service tee, I witnessed, fuser diaz. mole-shot service 85 ft. got 80 ft of 1" in. set curb valve, riser and meter. cover 36. tracer wire tested, continuity good. backfill tamped, sod put back. done 1430

Your notes say the mole-shot service was 85', and also that 80' of 1" was installed. Which is right?
