Chief’s report
Daily Report

Safety:
Arrived on site at 6:00 AM. A tailgate safety meeting covered pinch points, slips and trips in the mud, sideboom swing radius, and line of fire.

Site Conditions:
The right-of-way was muddy from overnight rain, with soft ground from Sta. 212+00 to Sta. 220+00. The contractor matted from Sta. 214+50 to Sta. 216+00 so the sidebooms could access the area. Standing water was present in the ditch around Sta. 215+00.

Coating Inspection:
Holiday detection was performed ahead of lowering. One (1) holiday was identified on the joint at Sta. 216+20. The coating crew repaired the holiday and re-jeeped the repair, with no holidays detected.

Lowering:
Spread 3 lowered the 12" steel pipe using three sidebooms. The ditch was dewatered at Sta. 215+00 prior to lowering, with discharge pumped to a filter bag. No turbid discharge was observed.
• Lowered 540' from Sta. 213+00 to Sta. 218+40
• Padding: 6" under the pipe
• Rock shield: Sta. 217+00 to Sta. 217+60

Backfill:
Spread 3 backfilled the lowered pipe. No rock was observed against the pipe.
• Backfilled 300' from Sta. 213+00 to Sta. 216+00

Departed site at 5:00 PM.

Personnel:
The crew was short two laborers, per General Foreman Daniels.
