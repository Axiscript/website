onsite 0545. jsa / tailgate w/ open range, 2 weld rigs + coating crew + lowering crew. hot work permit good. WPS R6 rev 3 checked.

12" x52 gathering line sta 142+50 to 151+10. heat nos 77812 and 78004 on the joints they welded today.

9 mainline welds today. welder 07: W-118 W-119 W-120 W-121 W-122. welder 12: W-123 W-124 W-125 W-126. vis all good, workmanlike. preheat 250 on W-122, was windy on that one. I witnessed root and hot on all but W-125, I was down at the foreign crossing when they ran the root on 125.

UT shot W-118 thru W-124, 5 pass, 2 rejects W-120 and W-121 porosity, cut out and rewelded same day, still welder 07. no UT yet on the 2 rewelds or on W-125 W-126. no RT today.

jeeped 900 ft at 2000V, 3 holidays found and repaired, coating batch D-4418. shrink sleeves on W-118 thru W-124, FieldBond batch FB26-1108-A. re-jeep on the 3 repairs still pending.

lowered in 600 ft, sta 142+50 to 148+50. padding sand 6in around the pipe. ditch 5ft avg, 4.5 at the swale. foreign 8" steel at 147+30, 24" sep, padded and timber marked. some rock at 146+10, extra pad.

rained 1300-1400, shut the welders down, coating stayed on the jeep till it slacked off. wind 15-20 mph out of the west most of the day.

off 1730. lowering crew still padding when I left.
Report out
Client Westfork Gathering Inspector Sam Ortega Contractor Open Range Construction

DPR Segment 4 11.10.2026

WO# 44821 Date 11/10/2026 Pipe spec 12" X52 steel Weather Rain 1:00–2:00 p.m., wind 15–20 mph
Location Segment 4

Safety

Inspector arrived on site at 5:45 a.m. A JSA/tailgate meeting was held with Open Range. Two weld rigs, a coating crew, and a lowering crew were present. The hot work permit was verified acceptable.

Welding

WPS R6, Revision 3, was reviewed. The crew completed nine (9) mainline welds, W-118 through W-126, on the 12" X52 gathering line from Sta. 142+50 to Sta. 151+10. Welder 07 welded W-118 through W-122, and welder 12 welded W-123 through W-126. Visual inspections confirmed no visible defects. Welds presented a workmanlike appearance. Wind was 15-20 mph out of the west most of the day. Preheat on W-122 was recorded at 250, and it was windy during that weld.

Root and hot passes were witnessed on all welds except W-125. The root pass on W-125 was run while the inspector was at the foreign crossing. Rain from 1:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m. shut down the welders.

NDE

UT was performed on W-118 through W-124. Five (5) welds were accepted, and two (2) were rejected. W-120 and W-121 were rejected for porosity. UT has not been performed on the two (2) rewelds or on W-125 and W-126. No RT was performed today.

Repairs

W-120 and W-121 were cut out and rewelded the same day by welder 07.

Coating Inspection

The crew jeeped 900' at 2,000V. Three (3) holidays were found and repaired. Shrink sleeves were installed on W-118 through W-124. The coating crew stayed on the jeep through the rain until it slacked off. Re-jeep of the three (3) repairs is pending.

Lowering

The crew lowered 600' of pipe from Sta. 142+50 to Sta. 148+50. Padding sand was placed 6" around the pipe. The ditch averaged 5' deep and was 4.5' deep at the swale. A foreign 8" steel line at Sta. 147+30 had 24" of separation and was padded and marked with timber. Rock was present at Sta. 146+10, and extra padding was placed.

The lowering crew was still padding when the inspector left. Inspector departed the site at 5:30 p.m.

Material

  • 12" X52 steel pipe – Heat Nos. 77812 and 78004 (joints welded today)
  • Coating – Batch D-4418
  • FieldBond – Batch FB26-1108-A

Midstream · tie-in & test

Notes in
onsite 0600. jsa w/ open range, tie-in crew + 1 weld rig + hydro crew. hot work permit good. one-call ticket 26-11044 checked, marks good.

tie-in today at sta 151+10 to the existing 12" westfork line at the valve set (valve V-14). line isolated, blinds in, gas-free confirmed, I was there for the sniff.

2 tie-in welds, TI-14 and TI-15, welder 07. WPS R6 rev 3. fit-up good, gap and hi-lo acceptable. preheat 250 both. I witnessed root, hot and caps. vis both good, workmanlike. UT same day on both, both accepted, no repairs.

coated both tie-in welds, shrink sleeves, coating batch D-4421, jeeped 2000V, no holidays.

hydro on the 12" section sta 142+50 to 151+10, 860 ft. they filled last night. test started 0930, 1480 psig, 8 hr hold, held, I watched the gauge, no pressure loss. test passed. depressured 1735, water back to the frac tanks.

blinds out after the test, V-14 back in service. bell hole backfilled, ROW dressed and rocked at the set. pulled timber mats. fixed silt fence at the low swale. no rain today, 42F, wind light.

off 1810.
Report out
Client Westfork Gathering Inspector Sam Ortega Contractor Open Range Construction

DPR Segment 4 11.12.2026

WO# 44821 Date 11/12/2026 Pipe spec 12" X52 steel Weather 42°F, light wind, no rain
Location Segment 4, valve set V-14

Safety

Inspector arrived on site at 6:00 a.m. No rain, 42°F, light wind. A JSA was held with Open Range, the tie-in crew, one (1) weld rig, and the hydro crew. The hot work permit was verified acceptable. One-call ticket 26-11044 was checked, and the marks were good.

Tie-In

The crew tied in at Sta. 151+10 to the existing 12" Westfork line at the valve set (valve V-14). The line was isolated, blinds were installed, and the work area was confirmed gas-free. The inspector witnessed the gas check.

Welder 07 made two (2) tie-in welds, TI-14 and TI-15, under WPS R6, Revision 3. Gap and hi-lo were acceptable. Preheat on both welds was recorded at 250. The inspector witnessed the root, hot, and cap passes. Visual inspections confirmed no visible defects. Welds presented a workmanlike appearance.

NDE

UT was performed on both tie-in welds the same day. Both welds were accepted, and no repairs were required.

Coating

Both tie-in welds were coated with shrink sleeves, coating batch D-4421. Holiday detection was performed at 2,000V, and no holidays were detected.

Hydrostatic Test

The 12" section from Sta. 142+50 to Sta. 151+10, 860', was filled the previous night. The test started at 9:30 a.m. at 1,480 psig with an 8-hour hold. The section held. The inspector watched the gauge, and no pressure loss was observed. Test passed. The section was depressured at 5:35 p.m., and the water was returned to the frac tanks. Blinds were removed after the test, and V-14 was returned to service.

Cleanup / Restoration

The bell hole was backfilled, and the ROW was dressed and rocked at the valve set. Timber mats were pulled. Silt fence was repaired at the low swale.

Inspector departed the site at 6:10 p.m.

1" pe sdr11 service to 412 elm st off the 4" pe sdr11 main. boxed out over the main, natural spoil, main looked good, no gouges. main cover 36.

saddle-fused service tee onto the main, I witnessed the whole fusion, fuser diaz. 1 fusion. melt pattern good, 10 min cool followed, I was there. punched the tee, I watched.

mole-shot service 85 ft. 85 ft of 1" sdr11 in. print line / mfg date 08/2026. tracer 12 taped to the top.

cover 36 at the main and 36 at the riser. set curb valve, box plumb, riser and meter set. efv in.

tracer wire tested, continuity good.

service pressure tested 90 psig for 15 min, held, I watched the gauge, no loss. test passed.

backfill tamped in lifts, warning tape in, sod put back, driveway boards pulled. homeowner was home, asked us to stay off the west side of the yard, dog in the fence. elm is one way 12-4, had a flagger 1000 to 1400.

Report out
Client Cedar Valley Gas Inspector Sam Ortega Contractor Lane Utility

DPR 412 Elm St 11.6.2026

WO# 55208 Date 11/6/2026 Pipe spec 1" PE SDR11
Location 412 Elm St Markout# 26-44107 Foreman Mike

Excavation / Installation

Inspector arrived on site at 7:00 a.m. A tailgate meeting was held with the foreman. Locates were on, and the marks were good. Elm St was one way 12-4, and a flagger was on site from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. The homeowner was home and asked the crew to stay off the west side of the yard because the dog was in the fence.

The crew installed a 1" PE SDR11 service to 412 Elm St off the 4" PE SDR11 main. The crew boxed out over the main. Spoil was natural. The main was observed with no gouges. The crew installed 85' of 1" SDR11 service by mole shot, and #12 tracer wire was taped to the top of the pipe. The curb valve was set with the box plumb, and the riser and meter were set. An excess flow valve was installed. The tracer wire was tested, and continuity was good.

Fusion / Tie-In

One (1) saddle fusion was performed to install a service tee onto the main by fuser Diaz. The inspector witnessed the entire fusion. The melt pattern was good. A 10-minute cooling period was followed and witnessed. The inspector watched the tee being punched.

Pressure Test

The service was pressure tested at 90 psig for 15 minutes and held. The inspector watched the gauge, and no pressure loss was observed. Test passed.

Backfill / Restoration

Cover measured 36" at the main and 36" at the riser. The crew backfilled and tamped in lifts. Warning tape was installed, sod was replaced, and the driveway boards were pulled. Work was completed at 2:30 p.m.

Personnel

The crew consisted of four personnel from Lane Utility.

Material

  • 1" PE SDR11 Pipe – MFG Date: 08/2026
