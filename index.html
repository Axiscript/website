Meet Chief

An assistant that sticks to what you wrote.

Chief turns rough field notes into organized report lines. He keeps every number and name exactly as you wrote it, and he doesn’t fill in what you didn’t say. Every report stays a draft until you review, edit, and approve it.

Example

You wrote: “240 ft of 4" pe in, 3 butt fusions saw 2, cover 36, backfill tamped”

Chief drafts: The crew installed 240' of 4" PE with 36" of cover. Three butt fusions were completed, two witnessed. Backfill was tamped.
