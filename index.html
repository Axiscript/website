Chief’s report
Daily Report

Safety:
Arrived on site at 6:30 AM. Met with Superintendent Mercer and General Foreman Holt at the staging yard. The JSA covered unloading equipment, clearing, overhead hazards, and heavy equipment. One-call tickets and ROW limits were reviewed.

Equipment:
The contractor began moving equipment in at approximately 7:00 AM and unloaded it at the staging area near Sta. 0+00. No concerns were noted at the equipment or staging area.
• Two (2) D6s
• Two (2) CAT 336s
• One (1) CAT 320
• 850L dozer
• 299 skid steer
• Forestry mulcher

Survey:
The survey crew staked the centerline and ROW limits and continued working ahead of the clearing crew. Existing utilities were marked near the access road.
• Centerline and ROW limits staked: Sta. 0+00 to Sta. 35+00
• Existing utilities marked near access road: Sta. 4+80

Site Conditions:
Ground was wet near Sta. 6+50, and the crew placed timber mats for access. No major rock was encountered.

Clearing/Grading:
Clearing began at approximately 8:30 AM at Sta. 0+00 and consisted mostly of brush and small timber. Larger trees around Sta. 11+00 were left for later. The dozers rough graded, and clearing and grading continued through the afternoon. Work was kept inside the staked ROW.
• Cleared approximately 1,250' ending at Sta. 12+50
• Grubbed first 600'
• Rough graded: Sta. 0+00 to Sta. 8+00

No stringing, ditching, welding, or pipe installation was performed today.

Erosion Control:
The environmental crew installed silt fence on the downslope side and straw wattles near the drainage. The environmental coordinator checked the installation, and no issues were noted. Erosion controls were installed before ground disturbance.
• Silt fence: 900' from Sta. 2+00 to Sta. 11+00
• Straw wattles near drainage: Sta. 9+75

Material:
The first pipe delivery arrived at 10:30 AM on two trucks. The pipe was unloaded to the laydown yard with an excavator and lifting attachments, then set on timber skids and chocked. Several joints were checked, and no visible coating damage was observed. Heat numbers and markings were present on the pipe, but the heat numbers were not recorded today. The equipment and material yard was secured.
• 40 joints of 12" X52 pipe, 0.375 wall, average 40' each
• 1,600' total

Departed site at 5:30 PM.
