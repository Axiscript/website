Chief’s report
Daily Report 11/11/2026
Client: Blackridge Midstream Resources LLC
Contractor: Summit Ridge Pipeline Constructors LLC
WO#: 2601847
Pipe: 12" API 5L X52, .375 WT

Safety:
Arrived on site at 6:30 AM. A JSA was covered addressing clearing, equipment movement, overhead hazards, and working on slopes. Met with Superintendent Mercer and General Foreman Holt.

Site Conditions:
Weather was clear early in the shift, and ground conditions were soft in low areas. The wet area near Sta. 6+50 remained, and timber mats stayed in place.

Survey:
The survey crew continued staking the centerline and ROW limits, working ahead of the clearing crews. A proposed access crossing was marked near Sta. 48+50.
• Staking: Sta. 35+00 to Sta. 62+00

Clearing/Grading:
The clearing crew started at Sta. 12+50. Larger timber around Sta. 11+00 from yesterday was removed. Scattered rock around Sta. 16+00 was cleaned up by an excavator. The crew continued grading and cleanup through the afternoon, and the contractor secured the ROW access and staging yard.
• Clearing: 1,850' from Sta. 12+50 to Sta. 31+00
• Grubbing: 1,600' from Sta. 6+00 to Sta. 22+00
• Rough grading: 1,150' from Sta. 8+00 to Sta. 19+50

Environmental Controls:
The environmental crew installed silt fence and added straw wattles at the drainage. Controls along the completed ROW were checked, and no visible deficiencies were noted.
• Silt fence: 1,100' from Sta. 11+00 to Sta. 22+00
• Straw wattles: drainage near Sta. 18+75

Stringing:
The stringing crew started at approximately 11:00 AM. Pipe was loaded at the laydown yard and strung along the graded ROW. The pipe was placed on skids clear of the equipment travel path. Pipe was checked after placement, and no visible coating damage was observed.
• Joints strung: 20
• Stringing: 800' from Sta. 0+00 to Sta. 8+00
No bending, welding, ditching, or pipe installation had been performed.
Departed site at 5:30 PM.

Material:
Three (3) additional trucks of 12" X52 steel pipe arrived at approximately 9:30 AM. The pipe was unloaded at the laydown yard, set on skids, and chocked. Coating was checked during unloading, and no visible coating damage was observed. Heat numbers and pipe markings were visible.
• Joints delivered: 60
• Wall thickness: .375
• Average joint length: 40'
• Delivered today: 2,400'
• Total onsite: 4,000'
