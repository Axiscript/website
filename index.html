Meet Chief

The chief who keeps your report straight.

In the field, Chief is the boss. He takes your rough notes and turns them into an organized report. He keeps every number and name exactly as you wrote it, and he doesn’t fill in what you didn’t say. Every report stays a draft until you review, edit, and approve it.

Midstream · gathering line
You wrote

onsite 0600 jsa w/ tie-in crew + 2 weld rigs. 12in gathering line sta 142+50 to 151+10. 9 mainline welds today, welders 07 and 12. ut shot 6, 2 cut out for porosity, rewelded. holiday test 900 ft, 3 holidays repaired. lowered in 600 ft, padding sand 6in. ditch 5ft avg, crossed foreign 8in at 147+30, 24in sep. rained 1300-1400 shut down. off 1730

Chief drafts
Safety

Inspector on site at 6:00 AM. JSA reviewed with the tie-in crew and two weld rigs.

Welding

Welders 07 and 12 completed nine mainline welds on the 12" gathering line between Sta. 142+50 and Sta. 151+10.

NDE

Six welds were shot by UT. Two were cut out for porosity and rewelded.

Coating

900' was holiday tested. Three holidays were found and repaired.

Lowering-in

600' was lowered in on 6" of sand padding. Average ditch depth was 5'.

Foreign crossing

The line crossed a foreign 8" line at Sta. 147+30 with 24" of separation.

Weather

Rain from 1:00 to 2:00 PM shut down work.

Closeout

Off site at 5:30 PM.

Chief flags

The two rewelds don’t have NDE results yet. Add them, or note they’re pending.

Distribution · service install
You wrote

onsite 0700. tailgate w/ mike, 4 man crew. 1" pe service to 412 elm st off the 4" pe main. saddle-fused service tee, I witnessed, fuser diaz. mole-shot service 85 ft. set curb valve, riser and meter. cover 36. tracer wire tested, continuity good. backfill tamped, sod put back. done 1430

Chief drafts
Safety

Inspector on site at 7:00 AM. Tailgate with Mike and a 4-man crew.

Installation

A 1" PE service was installed to 412 Elm St off the 4" PE main. The service was a mole-shot service for 85'.

Tie-in

A saddle-fused service tee was installed and witnessed. Fuser was Diaz.

Meter set

Curb valve, riser, and meter were set. Cover was 36".

Testing

Tracer wire was tested and continuity was good.

Restoration

Backfill was tamped and sod was put back.

Closeout

Off site at 2:30 PM.

Chief flags

The riser pressure test is not recorded. Add it, or note it’s pending.
